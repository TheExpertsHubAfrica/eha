Partnership Proposal 01 – 03
01 — Executive Summary

Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a revised performance-based partnership between The Experts Hub Africa (TEHA) and the technical partner responsible for building, maintaining and strengthening the organisation’s digital platform.

The platform was initially developed without a conventional upfront development fee. TEHA and the technical partner agreed instead on a GH₵500 commission per successfully processed client generated through the platform.

Since then, the solution has evolved into a multi-module business platform — supporting work applications, travel and study lead generation, staff administration, analytics, secure document handling and automated communications.

Proposed arrangement: GH₵1,000 per successfully processed client — a performance-based model aligned with business outcomes, covering the total digital infrastructure delivered, ongoing technical support and a structured continuity plan.
02 — Background

Background of the Existing Partnership

The original arrangement was founded on shared growth and mutual trust:

  • Full platform development responsibility was accepted without an upfront project fee.
  • TEHA’s costs were tied to real business results rather than hourly billing.
  • Significant investment was made in architecture, workflows, security and admin tools.

That model was appropriate at the early stage. The platform scope, operational dependency, and long-term technical responsibility have since expanded materially.

03 — Evolution

06 — Ongoing Support

Ongoing Technical Support & Maintenance

As technical partner, ongoing support includes:

  • Platform monitoring, stability and bug fixes
  • Support for admin and applicant technical issues
  • Maintenance of existing functionality
  • Reasonable optimisations and minor improvements
  • Technical guidance on roadmap and infrastructure decisions
  • Deployment and environment support (hosting, email, storage)
07 — Scope Boundaries

Scope Boundaries

Included

  • Stability and bug fixes
  • Minor UX improvements
  • Security and dependency maintenance
  • Agreed response-window support

Separate agreement

  • Major new modules (payments, portals, etc.)
  • Large redesigns or new product lines
  • New third-party integrations
  • Unlimited feature development

Principle: ongoing support keeps the platform healthy; substantial new development is scoped and agreed separately.

08 — Continuity Plan

Technical Continuity & Exit Plan

TEHA should never be operationally stranded if the technical partner steps back. A structured transition framework is proposed:

Phase Proposed approach
During partnership Documented, deployable codebase; TEHA-owned hosting and database where possible
Notice period 60–90 days written notice before disengagement
Handover Architecture guide, deployment checklist, admin guide, knowledge transfer sessions
Replacement support Help define role requirements and orient incoming developer (capped hours)
Post-exit Limited critical-issue support only (e.g. 30 days, capped — not indefinite)

10 — Justification

Strategic Justification

The increase reflects a shift from website builder to long-term technical partner responsible for a business-critical platform. TEHA still pays only on success — while avoiding the cost of a full-time developer, agency fees and unstructured manual operations.

Central message: GH₵1,000 is not compensation for a single feature. It is a revised performance partnership covering total digital infrastructure, ongoing support, and responsible continuity planning.

12 — Definitions

Recommended Contractual Definitions

Successfully processed client

Define a clear business milestone — e.g. placement confirmed, service fee received, or agreed advisory milestone completed — with no refund/cancellation within an agreed window (14–30 days).

Commission payable

Payable within an agreed period after TEHA confirms successful processing. Monthly reconciliation with 14-day dispute window.

Client scope

Clarify whether commission applies to all platform-originated clients or only referred clients. Recommend documenting the chosen model explicitly.

Exit & handover

60–90 days notice; capped handover hours (e.g. 40 hours transfer + 10 hours post-exit critical support within 30 days).
