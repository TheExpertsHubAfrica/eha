Partnership & Commission Agreement

This Partnership & Commission Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into on the date of the last signature below (“Effective Date”) by and between the parties identified below.

Party One — Client

The Experts Hub Africa (“TEHA”)

A Ghana-based organisation providing work abroad, study abroad, travel, visa assistance, and related advisory services to applicants.

Party Two — Developer

Stephen Gyan Bimpong (“Developer”)

Independent technical partner responsible for the design, development, deployment, and maintenance of the TEHA digital platform.

Recitals

WHEREAS:

  1. TEHA operates a client-facing digital platform for enquiries, applications, and service delivery across its listed service categories;
  2. The Developer has designed, built, and continues to support the platform without an upfront development fee, on the basis of a performance-linked commission arrangement;
  3. The parties wish to record their mutual understanding in writing, including the definition of a qualifying applicant, the commission rate, and each party’s ongoing obligations.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

01 — Definitions

Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise:

  • “Platform” means the TEHA website, admin dashboard, application workflows, document handling, email notifications, and related digital systems operated for TEHA’s business.
  • “Listed Services” means any service category published on the Platform at the time of an applicant’s engagement, including but not limited to work abroad placements, study abroad guidance, travel services, and visa assistance.
  • “Applicant” means an individual who submits an enquiry or application through the Platform for a Listed Service.
  • “Successful Applicant” means an Applicant who meets all of the following conditions:
    1. applied through the Platform;
    2. made the necessary payment required by TEHA for the relevant Listed Service; and
    3. has been successfully assisted by TEHA in that Listed Service, as confirmed in writing by an authorised TEHA representative.
  • “Commission” means the amount payable to the Developer for each Successful Applicant, as set out in Clause 4.
  • “Commission Period” means a calendar month, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

Clause 2

Developer’s Obligations

The Developer shall, during the term of this Agreement:

  • maintain, support, and reasonably improve the Platform in line with TEHA’s operational needs;
  • ensure the Platform remains secure, accessible, and fit for its intended business purpose, including application intake, admin operations, and client communications;
  • respond to critical technical issues within a reasonable timeframe and keep TEHA informed of material outages or security incidents;
  • provide TEHA with reasonable documentation and access credentials necessary for business continuity; and
  • not introduce third-party dependencies or infrastructure changes that materially impair TEHA’s ability to operate without prior notice to TEHA.

Major new modules, redesigns, or scope expansions beyond routine maintenance and agreed operational support shall be subject to separate written agreement between the parties.

Clause 3

TEHA’s Obligations

TEHA shall:

  • use the Platform as the primary channel for recording applicants originating from digital intake workflows covered by this Agreement;
  • maintain accurate records of Applicants, payments received, and service outcomes sufficient to verify Successful Applicants;
  • notify the Developer monthly of all Successful Applicants qualifying for Commission during the preceding Commission Period;
  • pay Commission due in accordance with Clause 4; and
  • designate an authorised representative to confirm Successful Applicant status and resolve Commission disputes in good faith.
Clause 4

Commission & Payment

Agreed rate: GH₵500 (Five Hundred Ghana Cedis) per Successful Applicant.
  1. Commission is earned only upon confirmation that an individual qualifies as a Successful Applicant under Clause 1.
  2. Commission applies to Successful Applicants regardless of which Listed Service they engaged, provided they applied through the Platform.
  3. TEHA shall pay all Commission due for a Commission Period within fourteen (14) calendar days after the end of that period, together with a written statement listing each qualifying Successful Applicant.
  4. Payment shall be made by bank transfer or mobile money to an account designated in writing by the Developer.
  5. If the Developer disputes an item on a Commission statement, TEHA and the Developer shall review the supporting records within fourteen (14) calendar days and adjust any confirmed underpayment or overpayment in the next payment cycle.
  6. No Commission is payable where an Applicant’s payment is refunded, reversed, or cancelled within thirty (30) days of TEHA’s confirmation of successful assistance, or where TEHA reasonably determines that the Applicant did not in fact receive the agreed service.

Clause 5

Intellectual Property

  1. The Developer retains ownership of pre-existing tools, libraries, and general technical know-how used in building the Platform.
  2. Upon full payment of all Commission due at the time of any agreed handover, TEHA shall receive a licence to use, operate, and host the Platform for its business purposes.
  3. TEHA owns its brand assets, content, client data, and business records. The Developer shall not use TEHA client data except as necessary to operate and support the Platform.
  4. Source code, deployment credentials, and technical documentation shall be maintained in a form that allows orderly transfer if the partnership ends, subject to any outstanding Commission being settled.
Clause 6

Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public business, financial, technical, and client information received from the other party, and shall use such information only for purposes of performing this Agreement. This obligation survives termination for a period of two (2) years, except where disclosure is required by law.

Clause 7

Term & Termination

  1. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party on sixty (60) days’ written notice to the other party.
  2. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if the breach is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice.
  3. Upon termination, TEHA shall pay all Commission earned for Successful Applicants confirmed before the effective termination date. The Developer shall provide reasonable handover assistance for up to forty (40) hours during the notice period.
  4. Clauses relating to Commission, confidentiality, intellectual property, and dispute resolution shall survive termination.
Clause 8

Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, consequential, or punitive loss. Each party’s total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Commission paid or payable to the Developer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except in cases of fraud or wilful misconduct.

Clause 9

General Provisions

  1. Independent contractor. The Developer acts as an independent contractor and not as an employee, agent, or partner of TEHA except as stated in this Agreement.
  2. Entire agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding the commission arrangement for the Platform and supersedes prior informal understandings on the same subject, unless supplemented by a later signed amendment.
  3. Amendments. Any change to the Commission rate or material terms must be in writing and signed by both parties.
  4. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana. Disputes shall first be resolved through good-faith negotiation; failing that, through mediation or the courts of Ghana.
  5. Notices. Formal notices under this Agreement shall be sent to the contact details recorded by each party at signing, or to any updated address given in writing.
