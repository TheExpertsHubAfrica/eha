Referral System Specification 01 – 02
01 — Overview

Executive Overview

The Agent & Ambassador Referral Management System is a strategic client acquisition channel for TEHA — not merely an additional website feature.

It enables accredited travel agents and motivated ambassadors to register on the platform, promote TEHA through unique referral links, bring potential clients into TEHA’s existing application and enquiry workflows and track performance through dedicated dashboards.

Administrators gain full visibility into the referral ecosystem — supporting partnership decisions, commission validation, marketing strategy and performance management.

02 — Objectives

Business Objectives

Objective Expected outcome
Scale client acquisition Grow beyond TEHA’s direct marketing reach through partner networks
Measure partner contribution Attribute applications and successful clients to the correct source
Maintain quality & trust Distinguish accredited agents from general ambassadors
Reduce manual tracking Replace informal referral claims with system-recorded attribution
Support commission decisions Provide data for fair, evidence-based partner rewards

Referral System Specification 03 – 04
03 — Agents

Agent Registration

Agents are individuals or organisations accredited by the appropriate government authority to engage in travel-related services.

Registration requirements

  • Complete registration form with personal or organisational details
  • Provide contact information and business identifiers as required
  • Upload a valid accreditation or registration certificate
  • Accept TEHA’s partner terms and referral policies

Approval workflow

  • Submission enters a pending review queue
  • Administrator reviews registration details and uploaded certificate
  • Account approved or rejected with reason recorded
  • Approved agents receive dashboard access and referral capabilities
Purpose of certificate requirement: To distinguish accredited travel agents from general referral partners and protect TEHA’s brand and compliance standards.
04 — Ambassadors

Ambassador Registration

Ambassadors are individuals who wish to promote TEHA and refer potential clients but may not hold formal travel-agent accreditation.

Registration requirements

  • Similar registration flow to agents (personal details, contact information)
  • No accreditation certificate required
  • Accept partner terms and referral policies

Approval workflow

  • Registration submitted for administrative review
  • Approved ambassadors receive dashboard access
  • Can generate and share referral links immediately after approval

Referral System Specification 05 – 06
05 — Referral System

Referral Links & Attribution

Unique referral links

Each approved agent and ambassador receives a unique referral identifier embedded in shareable links, enabling TEHA to track:

  • Visitors arriving via the partner’s link
  • Enquiries and applications originating from that referral
  • Conversion through to successful client outcomes

Attribution rules (recommended)

  • First-touch attribution within an agreed window (e.g. 90 days)
  • Referral recorded when a visitor first arrives via a valid partner link
  • Attribution persists through application submission and processing
  • Admin override available for disputed cases, with audit logging

Promotion channels

Partners may share links through:

  • Social media campaigns
  • WhatsApp and messaging groups
  • Professional networks and events
  • Email and community outreach

Agent & Ambassador Dashboards

  • Link clicks / visits
  • Enquiries generated
  • Applications submitted

Account management

  • View registration status
  • Update contact details
  • Re-upload certificate (agents)

Reporting

  • Date-range performance summary
  • Client/application list attributed to partner
  • Export summary (optional phase)

08 — Platform Integration

Integration with Existing TEHA Platform

The referral system will build on infrastructure already in place:

  • Application workflow — referred clients enter existing work-abroad application flows with attribution recorded at entry
  • Contact & enquiry forms — study and travel leads capture referral source
  • Reference numbers — applications retain traceable identifiers linked to partners
  • Analytics engine — referral events feed into existing reporting patterns
  • Admin RBAC — referral management permissions integrated with current role system
  • Audit logging — partner approvals, document access and attribution overrides logged
  • Secure document storage — agent certificates stored using existing upload infrastructure
  • Email notifications — registration confirmations, approvals and status updates via Resend

Referral System Specification 09 – 11
09 — Security

Data, Security & Compliance

  • Partner accounts protected by secure authentication (separate from admin and applicant flows)
  • Accreditation certificates stored privately with admin-only access
  • Personal data handled in line with TEHA’s existing privacy policy
  • Referral attribution data retained for agreed reporting and commission periods
  • Rate limiting and validation on registration and login forms
  • Audit trail for approval decisions and attribution overrides
